How to Play

Draw a card and read the scenario, situation, or question. Respond before revealing the Professional Approach.

Using the Digital Deck

Reveal / hide: Tap the white center of the card.
Next card: Tap the blue title area or blue card-number/footer area.
Controls: “Flip Card (Next)” does the same thing as tapping a blue area.

In Full Screen or mobile deck view, use the card touch zones when the navigation controls are hidden.

Scenario

Put yourself into the situation and respond as if the guest were standing in front of you. What actions would you take? What would you say? How would you approach the situation?

Bonvoy

Answer the knowledge question about the Marriott Bonvoy program.

Level Up

Statement cards: Improve or “level up” something an agent said.
Picture cards: Decide what is wrong in the image and explain how it might impact the guest.

After each card: Discuss what worked, what could be stronger, then reveal the Professional Approach and compare.
